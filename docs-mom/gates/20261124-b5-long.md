# B5-long gate — 20261124 (P1, size the escalation re-prefill prize)

## Question
When the thin lane has absorbed a long, unseen block and the conversation then
escalates to the heavy lane, how much does exact re-prefill on the heavy lane
cost? Determines whether cross-model translation (P4) is needed for the MVP.

## Scenario (three-turn conversation)
1. Turn 1: short, thin lane.
2. Turn 2: paste a block of L tokens (document / tool output), thin lane, short reply.
3. Turn 3: forced to the heavy lane.

Measure the heavy lane's prefill of the delta in turn 3.

## Sweep
- L in {512, 2k, 4k, 16k, 32k}.
- Five prompts per L, drawn from a fixed committed set.
- Config: arm B from P0 (stock lanes, TF_QWEN_REQUEST_STARTS=1).
- Also record the thin lane's prefill rate at each L, and peak memory.

## Metric
Heavy delta-prefill ms at p50, p90, p99 of the workload distribution,
interpolated from the L sweep.

## Pre-registered bar (close cross-model translation for the MVP / P4 not started)
BOTH must hold:
- p90 delta-prefill under 500 ms; AND
- p90 delta-prefill under 15% of the escalation turn's end-to-end time.
Otherwise P4 becomes eligible, and Derek decides.

## Workload distribution
From production logs (read-only): p50/p90/p99 of new tokens since the heavy
lane last saw the conversation, at escalation points. Production (:8000 on
spark-2d3b) is not on this host, so that extraction is deferred; the L sweep
itself still yields the interpolation numbers.

## Result (23 Nov 2026) — bar NOT met → P4-eligible
Single mom-serve at context 32768, parallel 1, arm-B env
(`TF_QWEN_REQUEST_STARTS=1`), 22/25 cases valid (2x polling timeout + 1
log-pairing misreport dropped; valid subset only). Run files:
`experiments/results/b5-long/run-01/`.

| L (target) | cases | p90 delta tokens | p90 heavy delta-prefill ms |
|---|---|---|---|
| 512 | 5 | 638 | 266 |
| 2k | 5 | 2397 | 489 |
| 4k | 5 | 4733 | 862 |
| 16k | 5 | 18786 | 3138 |
| 32k | 2 | 32284 | 5695 |

Overall: heavy delta-prefill p50 716 ms, p90 3277 ms, p99 5686 ms;
escalation-turn e2e p50 1034 ms (15% bar = 155 ms).

- Bar check: p90 < 500 ms? NO (3277). p90 < 15% of e2e? NO (3277 vs 155).
  → **P4 (approximate handoff) becomes eligible; Derek decides.**
- Interpretation: exact re-prefill is cheap below ~2k tokens (p90 <= 489 ms
  at 2k) and expensive above ~4k (>= 862 ms). For short-turn MVP traffic the
  bar would pass; it fails as soon as the thin lane absorbs multi-k
  documents. The escalation turn's e2e is itself dominated by this prefill,
  which is why the 15% sub-bar is structurally hard.
- Cost anatomy: the 35B lane carries a ~260 ms fixed prefill floor (600-token
  delta still costs 266 ms), then ~5.4-6k tok/s marginal (32k delta = 5.7 s).
  The 2B lane prefills at ~16k tok/s — heavy delta-prefill runs ~2.7-3x
  slower than thin, plus the floor.
- Workload distribution from production logs: NOT AVAILABLE on this host —
  :8000 here carries no mom traffic (spark-2d3b's :8000 is llama-server per
  the peer). Interpolation table above substitutes for the p50/p90/p99
  extraction; re-do when a router log exists.
